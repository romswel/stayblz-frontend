# Stayblz product identity and content

Current direction: 2026-11-05. This supersedes the earlier supplier-named reference catalogue. Publication is authorised separately by the owner; see PROJECT_NOTES.md for the release review and deployment evidence.

## What changed

The customer catalogue now describes Stayblz designs: Racecard, Detailed Racecard, Race Day Summary, Form Comparison, Horse History, Harness Racecard, Harness Form Guide and Harness Workspace. Report descriptions, features and guides have been rewritten for these purposes. This is a change in product scope, not a claim to own renamed supplier reports. Planned designs remain labelled as designs; unavailable reports, data and exports remain unavailable.

No supplier brand, publisher ratings, advertising copy or supplier product promises are used as Stayblz offers. Original code-built illustrations are retained as empty layout previews, not replicas of branded PDFs. Generic racing fields and common navigation patterns can guide an independently designed app; they do not grant access to somebody else's data or product.

Harness Workspace currently searches and sorts loaded published harness entries and horses, opens record details, and links to the existing private notebook. It does not calculate power ratings, automated picks, pedigree scores, trouble ratings or other supplier analysis. Harness Racecard and Harness Form Guide have track/date selection, original explanations, reading help, access-plan information and empty sample states. They have no copied single-card prices, day-pass promises or bundled access.

## Existing links and selections

Legacy report URLs redirect to the new catalogue with query parameters preserved, including signed-out entry through login. The redirect is a navigation aid, not a statement that products are equivalent. A signed-in redirected page explains that it does not provide access to the old outside product.

Old preview-cart selections are not relabelled or sold as new products. A versioned account-specific browser-tab store starts with an empty new cart and explains that selections must be chosen again. The old browser data is left intact for review/rollback. This does not touch orders or customer database records; real orders do not exist in this preview.

| PDFs, replays, rating feeds and paid analysis | Not copied, embedded, hotlinked or supplied | A specific permission/licence for the actual use and delivery. |
| Prices, payments, subscriptions and entitlements | Unavailable; cart is a selection preview | Own commercial terms and server-authorised payment/download flow. |
| New descriptive product names | Working design labels | No trademark clearance claim is made. Review final commercial branding before launch. |

Do not scrape an entire competing site, assume free-to-view means reusable, copy formulas or marketing descriptions, or turn another company's products into ours by changing the label. Keep source references in internal research notes; display third-party branding only when a real authorised integration requires accurate attribution.
