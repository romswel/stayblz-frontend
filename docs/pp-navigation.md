# PP navigation

The sidebar opens the PP section and keeps all report destinations available. Inside PP pages, a compact bar groups those same destinations under Thoroughbred, Harness and More. It sits below the page heading and breadcrumb. These are navigation links to existing pages, not additional content tabs or new routes.

For pages within a report:

- Keep the PP bar at the section level. Do not repeat every descendant in it.
- Add a breadcrumb for each real parent, for example Home > PPs > Premium PPs > Samples. Parent items link to their overview; the final item names the current page.
- Show a small local navigation only when real sibling pages exist within that report, such as Details and Samples. Keep unrelated reports in the PP dropdowns.
- Give direct links a Back to parent fallback. When returning to a listing, preserve its filters in the URL.
- On phones use simple inline lists or a chooser for long local navigation, rather than hover-only cascading menus.

The five requested Thoroughbred reports now use this structure for Pricing & tracks or Find a horse, Features, Sample or Free race, How to read, and subscription information where applicable. Samples have explicit missing-file states. Section-level destinations remain shared by sidebar and dropdown navigation. No paid files, real prices or purchases are available. See pp-content-and-purchase-flow.md for the current scope and integration boundaries.
